# Engineering Experiment

## Before execution

- State the claim and the decision it could change.
- Define the comparison, fixed conditions, and success criteria.
- Record known confounders and prohibited interpretation.

## After execution

- Preserve the raw result or exact command.
- Distinguish observation, interpretation, and decision.
- Report negative and inconclusive results.
- Do not generalize beyond the measured setup.
